基础版规则 Base Game Rules

获胜目标 Victory

起始放置 Initial Placement

先按座位顺序各放一座村庄和一条道路，再逆序放第二组。第二座村庄从每个相邻资源地块各获得一张资源。Place one settlement and road in seat order, then a second pair in reverse order. Your second settlement gives you one resource from each adjacent resource-producing hex.

任何两座村庄或城市之间至少空出一个路口。开始游戏后，新道路须连接自己的路网或建筑，新村庄须连接自己的道路；城市只能升级自己的村庄。Leave at least one empty intersection between any two settlements or cities. During play, new roads must connect to your network or buildings, new settlements to your roads, and cities must replace your own settlements.

回合与资源 Turns & Resources

每回合掷两颗骰子。对应数字的地块给所有相邻村庄一张资源、城市两张；之后可交替交易、建造，最后结束回合。沙漠和强盗所在的地块不生产资源。Roll two dice each turn. Matching hexes produce one resource for each adjacent settlement and two for each city, for all players. Then trade and build in any order before ending your turn. The desert and the robber's hex produce nothing.

建造费用与上限 Building Costs & Limits

每人最多有 15 条道路、5 座村庄和 4 座城市。升级城市会收回村庄棋子，之后可再次建造。Each player has 15 roads, 5 settlements and 4 cities. Upgrading to a city returns the settlement piece to your supply for reuse.

航海家另外每人有 15 艘船，船和道路分别计算上限。Seafarers adds 15 ships per player, with a separate limit from roads.

七点与强盗 Seven & the Robber

掷出 7 不生产资源。资源卡超过 7 张的玩家须弃掉一半，向下取整；发展卡不计入。全部弃牌完成后，当前玩家将强盗移到另一块陆地，并从该地块旁有建筑且有资源的对手中选择一人，随机偷一张资源。A roll of 7 produces no resources. Players holding more than 7 resource cards discard half, rounded down; development cards do not count. Once all discards are complete, move the robber to a different land hex and steal one random resource from an opponent with a building there and at least one resource.

交易 Trading

行动阶段可向指定玩家或所有玩家报价，双方同意才交换资源；其他玩家只能与当前回合的玩家交易。发展卡不能交易。During the action phase, offer a trade to one player or everyone. Resources change hands only by mutual agreement. Other players may trade only with the active player. Development cards cannot be traded.

银行用四张同种资源换一张；自己的村庄或城市连接普通港口时为 3:1，连接专用港口时，对应资源为 2:1。The bank exchanges four identical resources for one. A settlement or city at a generic port gives a 3:1 rate; a matching resource port gives a 2:1 rate for that resource.

发展卡 Development Cards

骑士（恶魔）、道路建设、丰收和垄断：购买当回合不能使用，从下一个自己的回合起，掷骰前或掷骰后均可使用，每回合合计最多一张。先完成卡牌效果，再继续回合；掷骰前用牌后仍须掷骰。Knight, Road Building, Year of Plenty and Monopoly are playable on a later turn of your own, before or after rolling. Use only one in total per turn. Resolve its effect first; playing before the roll does not replace the roll.

最长道路与最大骑士团 Longest Road & Largest Army

最长道路至少 5 段，最大骑士团至少使用 3 张骑士卡，各值 2 分。并列时原持有者保留；只有更长的道路或更多已使用的骑士才能夺取。最长道路按连续路线计算，分叉不相加，对手建筑会阻断路线。Longest Road requires at least 5 connected edges; Largest Army requires at least 3 played knights. Each awards 2 points. The holder keeps the award on a tie. Only a longer road or more played knights can take it. Count one continuous route, not branches; opponents' buildings interrupt it.

航海家的船、海盗、金矿与地图奖励另见「航海规则」及「本图规则」。For ships, the pirate, gold fields and scenario bonuses, see Sailing Rules and Map Rules.
