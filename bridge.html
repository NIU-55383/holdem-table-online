桥牌规则 Bridge Rules

搭档与目标 / Partners & goal

四人使用一副无大小王的 52 张牌，每人 13 张。南北一队，东西一队。A 最大，依次为 K、Q、J、10 至 2。先叫牌决定本副的将牌和目标墩数，再打完 13 墩。

Four players, 52 cards, 13 each. North–South and East–West are partners. Cards rank A, K, Q, J, 10 down to 2. The auction sets the trump suit and target; then all 13 tricks are played.

叫牌 / The auction

发牌人先叫，顺时针轮流。可以不叫（Pass），或报 1–7 阶及花色。同阶顺序为 ♣ < ♦ < ♥ < ♠ < 无将 NT；新叫牌必须更高。1 阶要赢 7 墩，4 阶要赢 10 墩，即「阶数 + 6」。无将表示没有将牌。

The dealer calls first, clockwise. Pass or bid a level (1–7) and denomination. Within a level: ♣ < ♦ < ♥ < ♠ < NT. Each bid must be higher. The target is level + 6 tricks: 1-level = 7; 4-level = 10. NT means no trumps.

对手最后叫出的定约可以加倍（X）；己方被加倍后可再加倍（XX）。双方之间的不叫不影响资格，新的更高叫牌会取消加倍。有人叫牌后连续三人不叫，定约成立；开头四人都不叫，本副零分结束。最后叫牌一方中，最早叫过最终花色的人为庄家，不一定是最后叫牌的人。

Double (X) an opponent's current bid; redouble (XX) your side's doubled bid. Intervening passes do not prevent either. A new bid cancels doubles. Three passes after a bid end the auction; four opening passes pass out the board for zero. Declarer is the first player on the winning side who bid the final denomination.

点数与叫牌提示 / HCP & bidding hints

  • 开局前可选，默认关闭。房主开启后，每位玩家在叫牌期间只看到自己的点数与建议；下一副、刷新及存档续局会保留设置。Optional before the start and off by default. When enabled by the host, each player sees their own points and guidance during the auction. The setting persists across boards, reloads and saved games.

点数怎么算 / Counting your points

  • 每张 A 算 4 点、K 算 3 点、Q 算 2 点、J 算 1 点，10 到 2 都是 0 点。只加你自己 13 张牌的大牌点；一副 52 张牌总共 40 点。Each A = 4, K = 3, Q = 2, J = 1; 10 through 2 = 0. Add the high-card points (HCP) in your own 13 cards. The whole deck has 40 HCP.
  • 大牌点用来估计牌力，不是本副得分，也不是保证能赢的墩数。提示另列 ♠、♥、♦、♣ 各有几张；不会把长套点或短套点混入大牌点。HCP estimate strength, not the board score or guaranteed tricks. Suit lengths are shown separately; length or shortage points are not added to the HCP total.
  • 本馆使用简化自然叫牌：高花为 ♥/♠，低花为 ♣/♦。开叫 1♥/1♠ 通常至少五张；1♣/1♦ 可能只有三张。无将后的花色叫牌表示真实花色，不使用斯台曼或转移叫。The club uses simplified natural bidding: majors are hearts/spades, minors clubs/diamonds. Opening 1♥/1♠ normally shows at least five cards; 1♣/1♦ may have three. Suit responses to notrump are natural, with no Stayman or transfers.

还没人开叫时 / When no one has opened

  • 0–11 点：通常先不叫（Pass）。有较好的六张 ♦/♥/♠ 长套、约 6–10 点时，可考虑弱二开叫；七张长套可考虑三阶阻击。不要只凭点数阻击，有局时更要谨慎。0–11 HCP: usually pass. With a sound six-card diamond/heart/spade suit and about 6–10 HCP, a weak two may be suitable; seven cards may justify a three-level preempt. Suit quality and vulnerability matter.
  • 12–21 点：通常可从一阶花色开叫。优先五张高花；没有五张高花时选择低花。下面的均型无将和强牌开叫优先处理。12–21 HCP: normally open one of a suit, preferring a five-card major, otherwise a minor. The balanced-notrump and strong-hand cases below take priority.
  • 15–17 点且均型：开叫 1NT。均型指四门花色张数为 4-3-3-3、4-4-3-2 或 5-3-3-2；没有单张或缺门。15 点但牌型不均衡，不要照搬 1NT。15–17 HCP and balanced: open 1NT. Balanced shapes are 4-3-3-3, 4-4-3-2 and 5-3-3-2, with no singleton or void. An unbalanced 15-count does not automatically open 1NT.
  • 20–21 点且均型：开叫 2NT。均型 12–14 或 18–19 点通常先开一阶花色，再根据搭档应叫决定后续叫法。20–21 HCP and balanced: open 2NT. With balanced 12–14 or 18–19, normally open one of a suit and plan the rebid after partner responds.
  • 22 点起：本馆通常用强 2♣，并不表示梅花长；搭档通常先应 2♦ 等待。为了与本馆机器人一致，均型 25–27 点采用直接开叫 3NT。这是本馆的简化约定。22+ HCP: the club normally uses a strong 2♣, which does not promise clubs; partner usually waits with 2♦. Balanced 25–27 opens 3NT to match the club bots. This is a simplified club agreement.

搭档已经开叫时 / Responding to partner

  • 搭档开一阶花色，且对手没有插叫或加倍：0–5 点通常不叫；6 点起通常应叫。能在一阶报出四张以上的新花色时可考虑自然应叫；二阶新花色通常需要至少 10 点及足够长度。After partner opens one of a suit with no opponent bid or double: usually pass with 0–5 HCP and respond with 6+. Consider a natural four-card-or-longer new suit at the one-level; a new suit at the two-level normally needs 10+ HCP and sufficient length.
  • 搭档开 1♥/1♠，你有至少三张支持：约 6–9 点可加到二阶，10–12 点可邀到三阶，13 点起可考虑四阶成局。配合后的短套会影响牌力，不能把这个表当成只看大牌点的硬规则。After 1♥/1♠ with at least three-card support: about 6–9 points can raise to two, 10–12 invite at three, and 13+ consider game at four. Shortages affect value after a fit; these are not rigid HCP-only rules.
  • 搭档开 1NT（15–17 点），对手没干扰、你也没有更合适的长高花：0–7 点通常不叫；8–9 点可叫 2NT 邀请搭档；10–15 点通常可叫 3NT。更强时要评估满贯，不能直接套用 3NT。After an uncontested 1NT (15–17), without a more suitable long major: 0–7 HCP usually pass; 8–9 invite with 2NT; 10–15 usually bid 3NT. Stronger hands require slam assessment rather than an automatic 3NT.
  • 对手争叫、加倍，或已经进入再叫时，要结合已叫出的花色和双方牌力判断。提示会说明当前情况；无法可靠判断时，不硬给一个叫牌。搭档的点数只依据公开叫牌估计。After interference, doubles or rebids, use the auction and partnership strength. Hints explain the context and omit a specific call when uncertain. Partner strength is estimated only from public calls.

几分可以考虑成局 / Combined strength for game

  • 这里说的是你和搭档的合计牌力，不是你一个人的点数：约 25–26 点可考虑 3NT 或 4♥/4♠；高花定约通常还需要两人合计至少八张将牌。These are partnership totals, not your points alone: about 25–26 points can support 3NT or 4♥/4♠; a major-suit contract normally also needs at least eight combined trumps.
  • 5♣/5♦ 通常约需 29 点；小满贯常参考约 33 点，大满贯约 37 点。长套、短套、将牌配合、A 和 K 的位置都会改变判断，达到分数不保证能完成定约。Five of a minor often needs about 29 points; roughly 33 for a small slam and 37 for a grand slam. Length, shortages, fit and controls can change the assessment; reaching a threshold never guarantees success.
  • “有局”不改变 A/K/Q/J 的点数，但宕约罚分更高，成局与满贯奖励也更高。叫牌建议仅供你判断，仍需自己点击叫牌按钮。Vulnerability does not change HCP. It increases undertrick penalties and game/slam bonuses. Guidance is advisory; you still choose and submit every call yourself.

首攻、明手与出牌 / Lead, dummy & play

庄家左手的防家首攻。第一张牌出完，庄家的搭档摊开全部手牌，成为明手，由庄家替明手选牌。庄家与明手仍按原座位顺序出牌。明手玩家不能自行出牌。

The defender to declarer's left makes the opening lead. Dummy, declarer's partner, then exposes their hand. Declarer chooses cards for both hands, each at its usual turn. The dummy player does not play independently.

每墩四人各出一张，必须跟首张牌的花色。没有该花色时可出任意牌，也可出将牌「将吃」，但不强制。若有将牌，最大的将牌赢；否则首引花色中最大的牌赢。赢墩者领出下一墩。庄家与明手的墩数相加，防守方同理。

Each trick contains one card per seat, clockwise. Follow the led suit if you can. If void, play any card, including a trump; trumping is optional. Highest trump wins, otherwise highest card of the led suit. The winner leads next. Partners' tricks count together.

本桌计分与局况 / Scoring & vulnerability

本桌每副独立计分，采用定约桥牌的复式计分表；不累计橡皮桥局分，也不换算 IMP 或比赛分。每场 4、8 或 16 副，累计分高的一队获胜。发牌人每副顺移，局况按标准 16 副循环。红色标出的「有局」会提高成局、满贯奖励和宕约罚分，与本场领先与否无关。

Each board uses duplicate contract scoring, with no rubber carryover, IMPs or matchpoints. Play 4, 8 or 16 boards; the partnership with the higher total wins. Dealer rotates and vulnerability follows the standard 16-board cycle. Vulnerable (red) means larger game/slam bonuses and penalties, unrelated to the match lead.

完成定约：只算六墩以上、定约要求的墩。♣/♦ 每墩 20 分；♥/♠ 每墩 30 分；NT 第一墩 40 分，此后每墩 30 分。加倍乘 2，再加倍乘 4。定约墩分不到 100，另奖 50；达到 100 即成局，另奖无局 300 / 有局 500。超墩不影响是否成局。

Made contract: count contracted tricks above six. ♣/♦ = 20 each; ♥/♠ = 30 each; NT = 40 for the first, then 30. Double these points for X, quadruple for XX. Below 100 contract points: +50 partscore bonus. At least 100: +300 non-vulnerable / +500 vulnerable game bonus. Overtricks do not qualify a contract for game.

完成 6 阶小满贯，额外奖无局 500 / 有局 750；完成 7 阶大满贯，额外奖 1000 / 1500。完成加倍定约另奖 50，再加倍另奖 100。未加倍的超墩按本花色每墩分值算（NT 为 30）；加倍超墩每墩无局 100 / 有局 200，再加倍再乘 2。

Made small slam (6-level): add 500 non-vulnerable / 750 vulnerable; grand slam (7-level): add 1000 / 1500. Making a doubled contract adds 50; redoubled adds 100. Undoubled overtricks score the suit's normal value (NT = 30). Doubled overtricks: 100 non-vulnerable / 200 vulnerable each; twice that if redoubled.

未完成定约叫「宕约」，只有防守方得分。未加倍：每少一墩，无局罚 50 / 有局罚 100。加倍无局：第一墩 100，第二、三墩各 200，以后各 300；加倍有局：第一墩 200，以后各 300。再加倍罚分再乘 2。

If declarer goes down, only the defenders score. Undoubled: 50 per undertrick non-vulnerable, 100 vulnerable. Doubled non-vulnerable: 100 for the first, 200 each for the second and third, 300 each thereafter. Doubled vulnerable: 200 for the first, 300 each thereafter. Redoubled penalties are twice the doubled amounts.

机器人与房间 / Bots & room

机器人采用自然叫牌：通常 12 点开叫、五张高花，均型 15–17 点开 1NT、20–21 点开 2NT；22 点以上可强 2♣，搭档先以 2♦ 等待，再自然叫牌。弱二阶 ♦/♥/♠ 通常为六张套、6–10 点；三阶阻击通常为七张套。A/K/Q/J 分别算 4/3/2/1 点。无将后的花色应叫为自然叫，不使用斯台曼或转移叫。机器人根据叫牌、明手及已出牌判断余牌。

Bots use natural bidding: usually 12+ HCP to open, five-card majors, balanced 15–17 for 1NT and 20–21 for 2NT. Strong 2♣ shows 22+; partner waits with 2♦, followed by natural rebids. Weak 2♦/♥/♠ usually shows six cards and 6–10 HCP; three-level preempts usually show seven. A/K/Q/J count 4/3/2/1 HCP. Suit responses to NT are natural, without Stayman or transfers. Bots infer remaining cards from the auction, dummy and played cards.

房主可以补机器人；有人被移出时保留其手牌并暂停，补位后继续。两分钟自动托管默认关闭，由房主统一设置。

Hosts can add bots. Removing a seat preserves its hand and pauses play until replaced. Two-minute idle auto-play is off by default and controlled by the host.
